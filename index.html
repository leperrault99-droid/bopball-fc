HOW TO PLAY
Score more goals than the other beans. There are no fouls and no out-of-bounds — the fence is electric, use it.

With the ball: J pass (hold U for a lob) · K shoot — hold to charge 3 levels, charged shots can stun the keeper · L deke past tacklers.
Without the ball: J switch bean · K slide tackle (from behind = you both eat turf) · L BIG HIT — flatten anyone, but off-ball hits cost you recovery time.
One-timer: pass, then press K just before your teammate receives — instant volley the keeper can barely track.
A perfect untouched pass gives your receiver a glowing power boost. Body-check people into the fence for bonus zap.
Gamepad: stick move · A pass · X shoot · B deke/hit · LB lob · RT sprint.
PAUSED
